Postdoctoral researcher specialising in structural health monitoring, Bayesian inference, and reliability analysis for marine structures. Expertise in developing probabilistic frameworks for damage detection, optimal sensor placement, and decision-theoretic maintenance strategies with applications to ship hull structures and wind-assisted propulsion systems.

Current research focuses on value of information analysis for condition-based maintenance.

Research interests span structural health monitoring (SHM), Bayesian inference & uncertainty quantification, machine learning for structural analysis, ship hull structural reliability, wind-assisted propulsion systems, digital twins for marine structures, condition-based maintenance optimisation, and fatigue & fracture mechanics.
