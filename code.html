Research Projects

Wind-Assisted Propulsion Systems (WAPS) Digital Twin

2024-Present | Lead Researcher

Development of a digital twin framework for quantifying net WAPS power contribution and designing optimal risk-based inspection strategies. The project integrates multiple data modalities and explores graph neural networks for subsystem interface modelling.

Collaboration with NTUA, TU Delft, MARIN, Ocean Wings, and DNV.

Digital Twins Graph Neural Networks Risk-Based Inspection Marine Engineering

dTHOR - Digital Ship Structural Health Monitoring

2022-2024 | Research Engineer | European Defense Fund

Development of Bayesian approaches for structural deterioration monitoring and stochastic fatigue analysis frameworks for naval structures. Contributed to deliverables on linear damage accumulation and fracture mechanics approaches.

Multi-national defence consortium project focused on advanced SHM techniques for naval vessels.

Structural Health Monitoring Bayesian Inference Fatigue Analysis Naval Structures
